# About

Open Finance Academy is for anyone interested in finance and seeking financial freedom. It makes financial technology easier to understand and helps people learn how to use and build it responsibly.

We believe everyone has the right to pursue financial freedom and to have the tools to avoid financial exploitation and repression. That means sharing knowledge openly, examining the systems that shape financial lives, and building technology that protects people’s choices, privacy, and agency.

Too many financial education services place essential knowledge behind paywalls, excluding people with lower incomes and historically underrepresented communities. Open Finance Academy is committed to making practical financial education freely accessible, welcoming, and useful to everyone.

The curriculum will grow through community contributions, practical examples, and open discussion.

## Not financial advice

Open Finance Academy and its contributors are not financial advisors, and nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Content here is educational only, meant to help you understand financial concepts, systems, and tools so you can make more informed decisions on your own.

Wherever possible, we link out to reputable primary sources — regulators, central banks, academic research, and established financial institutions — so you can go deeper than any single article on this site. We encourage you to consult a qualified, licensed financial professional before making financial decisions, since a personalized recommendation depends on your specific circumstances in a way that general education cannot.
