# Economic Calendar

Use an economic calendar to see scheduled releases, policy decisions, and other events that may help put financial-market news in context. Calendar providers can differ in their coverage, time zones, event labels, and estimates, so check the details on the provider's own site.

## Our curated calendar

**Planned:** Open Finance Academy will add its own curated economic calendar in the future. It is intended to highlight important releases and decisions, explain what each event measures, and link to authoritative primary sources. Until then, use the external calendars above and verify dates, times, and figures with the relevant institution.
