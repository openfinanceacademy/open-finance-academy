# Financial Empowerment Grant Program

The Financial Empowerment Grant Program provides matched-savings grants to individuals building financial literacy through Open Finance Academy's curriculum, helping them start an emergency fund or a first investment account.

<span class="project-status">Starting up</span>

## How it works

1. **Learn** — complete a defined set of lessons from the [Knowledge]({{ '/knowledge/' | relative_url }}) library covering budgeting, saving, and the basics of investing.
2. **Save** — open a savings or custodial investment account and contribute what you can toward a goal (emergency fund, retirement starter account, or first taxable brokerage account).
3. **Apply** — submit an application scored against a published, objective rubric: financial need, lesson completion, and consistency of saving. No exceptions are made outside the published criteria.
4. **Match** — approved applicants receive a matching grant, up to a set cap, deposited toward the account they opened.

This structure is modeled on established Individual Development Account (IDA) programs, which pair matched savings with financial education and have a long track record with U.S. regulators and community-development funders.

## Why a matched grant, not a handout

Matching a saver's own contribution reinforces the habit the education program teaches, and it lets a modest amount of grant funding support many more participants than a lump-sum gift would.

## Funding

Grants are funded from realized returns on Open Finance Academy's fiscally-sponsored investment pool, not from principal — see [About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) for the organization's approach to funding its programs.

## Status

This program is in the design phase. The scoring rubric, award caps, and application process are being finalized before the first funding cycle opens. Follow the [Blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}) for updates, or see [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) for other programs.
