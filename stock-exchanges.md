# Stock Exchanges

Explore major stock exchanges around the world. Each exchange name links to its official website, where you can find listed companies, market information, and trading calendars.

This directory follows the 20 entries in Wikipedia's [List of major stock exchanges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_major_stock_exchanges), consulted September 13, 2026. Entries are arranged alphabetically. Some represent an exchange group operating several markets.

## Sources and market data

- [Wikipedia: List of major stock exchanges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_major_stock_exchanges) — the source list, including its dated market-size estimates and trading-hours table.
- [World Federation of Exchanges: Market Statistics](https://focus.world-exchanges.org/issue/february-2025/market-statistics) — historical statistics, with links to other monthly editions for comparing exchanges over time.
- [Euronext: Choosing a Market](https://www.euronext.com/en/listing/raise-capital/how-go-public/choosing-market) and [Nasdaq: European Markets](https://www.nasdaq.com/products/european-markets) — details of the European markets grouped in the directory.

For current opening hours and holidays, follow the official exchange links above to their trading calendars.
