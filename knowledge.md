## [Money and financial fundamentals]({{ '/knowledge/money-and-financial-fundamentals/' | relative_url }})

The core concepts used throughout finance.

- Money, currencies, interest, and compounding
- Time value of money, present value, and discounting
- Inflation, purchasing power, and real versus nominal returns
- Risk, return, liquidity, and opportunity cost

## [Personal finance and financial wellbeing]({{ '/knowledge/personal-finance-and-financial-wellbeing/' | relative_url }})

Managing money through everyday decisions and major life changes.

- Budgeting, cash flow, saving, and emergency funds
- Managing debt and understanding credit reports and scores
- Financial goals, retirement planning, and pensions
- Estate planning, inheritance, and financial decision-making habits

## [Accounting and financial analysis]({{ '/knowledge/accounting-and-financial-analysis/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: follow a small shop through its first month to connect financial statements, calculate ratios, and understand the limits of the numbers.

- Balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements
- Accrual accounting, revenue, expenses, assets, and liabilities
- Financial ratios, profitability, and business performance
- Financial reporting, audit, and the limits of accounting measures

## [Economics and the financial system]({{ '/knowledge/economics-and-the-financial-system/' | relative_url }})

How households, businesses, institutions, and policy interact.

- Supply and demand, incentives, and business cycles
- Central banks, monetary policy, and money creation
- Employment, growth, inflation, and economic indicators
- Financial institutions, systemic risk, and financial crises

## [Banking, credit, and lending]({{ '/knowledge/banking-credit-and-lending/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: compare accounts, follow loan repayments, assess borrowing costs, and understand credit decisions and repayment difficulties.

- Banks, credit unions, deposits, and deposit protection
- Consumer loans, mortgages, and business lending
- Interest rates, loan terms, collateral, and underwriting
- Credit risk, defaults, restructuring, and bankruptcy

## [Payments and money movement]({{ '/knowledge/payments-and-money-movement/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: follow a payment from payer to payee, compare card fees, and calculate the true cost of sending money abroad.

- Cards, bank transfers, direct debits, and instant payments
- Payment processing, clearing, and settlement
- Remittances, cross-border payments, and foreign exchange fees
- Fraud prevention, disputes, and payment security

## [Investing and portfolio management]({{ '/knowledge/investing-and-portfolio-management/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: connect investments to goals, compare funds, calculate portfolio results, and explore rebalancing and costs.

- Diversification, asset allocation, and rebalancing
- Funds, exchange-traded funds, and active versus passive investing
- Investment research, valuation, and due diligence
- Performance measurement, fees, taxes, and behavioral biases

## [Financial markets and instruments]({{ '/knowledge/financial-markets-and-instruments/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: compare instruments, interpret bond yields and trading quotes, and work through orders, derivatives, and leverage.

- Stocks, bonds, money markets, and commodities
- Futures, forwards, options, and swaps
- Exchanges, brokers, market makers, and trading orders
- Price discovery, market liquidity, leverage, and margin

## [Corporate finance and business funding]({{ '/knowledge/corporate-finance-and-business-funding/' | relative_url }})

Learn how businesses forecast cash, compare debt and equity, evaluate projects, and allocate capital. Includes worked examples of ownership dilution, cost of capital, project value, and cash runway.

- Capital budgeting, forecasting, and project evaluation
- Cost of capital, capital structure, and debt versus equity
- Working capital, treasury, and cash management
- Startup funding, venture capital, mergers, and acquisitions

## [Insurance and risk management]({{ '/knowledge/insurance-and-risk-management/' | relative_url }})

Learn to identify financial risks, compare insurance coverage, and calculate retained costs. Includes worked examples of property claims, health-plan cost sharing, deductible choices, and business stress testing.

- Life, health, property, and liability insurance
- Premiums, deductibles, exclusions, and claims
- Credit, market, liquidity, and operational risk
- A worked household stress test
- Planned deeper lessons: hedging, actuarial methods, and reinsurance

## [Real estate and alternative investments]({{ '/knowledge/real-estate-and-alternative-investments/' | relative_url }})

Learn how property and alternative investments generate returns, and compare borrowing, fees, valuation, and withdrawal terms. Includes worked rental-property examples and explained exercises.

- Property valuation, rental income, and real estate financing
- Real estate investment trusts and infrastructure
- Private equity, private credit, and hedge funds
- Illiquidity, fees, access, and due diligence

## [Public finance and taxation]({{ '/knowledge/public-finance-and-taxation/' | relative_url }})

How governments raise, spend, and borrow money.

- Government budgets, public services, and fiscal policy
- Income, consumption, property, and capital gains taxes
- Sovereign and municipal debt
- Tax systems, public pensions, and social insurance

## [International finance]({{ '/knowledge/international-finance/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: convert currencies, calculate overseas investment results, connect trade and financial flows, and examine sovereign risk and development finance.

- Exchange rates and currency markets
- Balance of payments, trade, and international capital flows
- Currency risk and international investing
- Sovereign risk, emerging markets, and development finance

## [Financial law, ethics, and consumer protection]({{ '/knowledge/financial-law-ethics-and-consumer-protection/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: compare fees, recognize conflicts and scams, examine privacy and fair access, and document complaints. Legal examples identify their jurisdiction and review date.

- Financial regulation, supervision, and compliance
- Consumer rights, disclosures, and dispute resolution
- Fraud, scams, predatory practices, and conflicts of interest
- Financial privacy, access, and responsible product design

## [Financial technology and open finance]({{ '/knowledge/financial-technology-and-open-finance/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: connect accounts through open banking, compare data-access methods, and weigh automated tools like robo-advisors.

- Open banking, data portability, and consent
- APIs, financial data standards, and interoperability
- Digital identity, authentication, and cybersecurity
- Financial applications, automation, and responsible use of AI

## [Blockchain and decentralized finance]({{ '/knowledge/blockchain-and-decentralized-finance/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: follow blockchain transactions, distinguish wallets and custody, calculate pool trades and collateral thresholds, and examine token returns and protocol risks.

- Blockchains, wallets, custody, and smart contracts
- Digital assets, stablecoins, and tokenization
- Decentralized exchanges, lending, and governance
- Protocol security, economic risks, and regulatory questions

## [Quantitative finance and financial data]({{ '/knowledge/quantitative-finance-and-financial-data/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: estimate uncertain outcomes, summarize returns, examine correlation, build simple forecasts, check data quality, and backtest a strategy honestly.

- Probability, statistics, and financial modeling
- Time series, econometrics, and forecasting
- Data quality, reproducible analysis, and visualization
- Backtesting, model risk, and the limits of predictions

## [Sustainable finance and financial inclusion]({{ '/knowledge/sustainable-finance-and-financial-inclusion/' | relative_url }})

An introductory lesson is available: calculate the cost of exclusion, compare small loans, explore community finance and microfinance, and examine climate risk, sustainable-investing labels, and accountability.

- Access to banking, credit, and affordable financial services
- Community finance, cooperatives, and microfinance
- Climate risk, sustainable investing, and impact measurement
- Financial exclusion, unequal outcomes, and accountability
