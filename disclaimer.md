# Disclaimer

Open Finance Academy and its contributors are not financial advisors, and Open Finance Academy is not an accredited educational provider. Nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and the site does not provide accredited courses, qualifications, or personalized recommendations.

Content here is general educational information intended to help you understand financial concepts, systems, and tools so you can make more informed decisions on your own. It may not fit your circumstances, and financial rules and conditions vary by jurisdiction and change over time.

Wherever possible, we link to reputable primary sources, including regulators, central banks, academic research, and established financial institutions. Consult a qualified, licensed professional before making financial decisions that depend on your specific circumstances.
