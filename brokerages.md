# Brokerages

Explore major brokerages and investment platforms around the world, with links to their official websites.

This directory is a selection of established international firms and prominent regional providers, including online brokers and brokerage services offered by banks. It is not an exhaustive list or a ranking. Locations indicate a firm's home market or regional presence; they do not mean everyone in that region can open an account. Available accounts, investments, and fees depend on your country and the local provider.
