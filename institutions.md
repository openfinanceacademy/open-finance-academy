# Institutions

Meet the institutions and public agencies that shape money, government finances, development, and financial markets around the world, including the Federal Reserve, the U.S. Treasury, and the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

This selected directory considers international influence, economic reach, and regional importance. It is grouped by role, with no size ranking: a central bank, a government ministry, and a development lender do different jobs. Each institution's name links to its official website or an explanation of its work.

*Directory updated: September 13, 2026.*

## Central banks and monetary authorities

Central banks guide monetary policy: decisions that influence the cost and availability of money. They also help keep financial systems and payments functioning. Their responsibilities differ by country.

| Institution | Country or region | Main role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Federal Reserve System (the Fed)](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed.htm) | United States | Conducts U.S. monetary policy, supervises certain banks, and supports financial stability and dollar payments. |
| [European Central Bank (ECB)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/ecb/all-about-us/html/index.en.html) | Euro area | Works with national central banks to conduct monetary policy for countries using the euro; also supervises banks within European banking supervision. |
| [Bank of England (BoE)](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/) | United Kingdom | The UK's central bank, responsible for monetary and financial stability. |
| [People's Bank of China (PBC)](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/) | China | China's central bank, responsible for monetary policy and the renminbi monetary system. |
| [Bank of Japan (BoJ)](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/) | Japan | Conducts monetary policy and supports the yen payment and financial systems. |
| [Reserve Bank of India (RBI)](https://www.rbi.org.in/) | India | India's central bank, with responsibilities for monetary policy, currency, banking, and payments. |
| [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/) | Canada | Canada's central bank, supporting monetary and financial stability. |
| [Central Bank of Brazil (BCB)](https://www.bcb.gov.br/en) | Brazil | Brazil's central bank, responsible for monetary policy and a sound financial system. |
| [Bank of Mexico (Banco de México)](https://www.banxico.org.mx/) | Mexico | Mexico's central bank, responsible for monetary policy and the peso monetary system. |
| [Swiss National Bank (SNB)](https://www.snb.ch/en) | Switzerland | Conducts monetary policy for the Swiss franc. |
| [Bank Indonesia (BI)](https://www.bi.go.id/en/) | Indonesia | Indonesia's central bank, responsible for rupiah stability and payment-system policy. |
| [Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)](https://www.mas.gov.sg/) | Singapore | Singapore's central bank and financial regulator. |
| [Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/) | Saudi Arabia | Saudi Arabia's central bank, supporting monetary and financial stability. |
| [South African Reserve Bank (SARB)](https://www.resbank.co.za/) | South Africa | South Africa's central bank, responsible for monetary policy and financial stability. |
| [Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA)](https://www.rba.gov.au/) | Australia | Australia's central bank, responsible for monetary policy and contributing to payment-system and financial stability. |

For a wider international directory, see the [BIS list of member central banks](https://www.bis.org/about/organisation/members).

## Treasuries and finance ministries

These government departments help manage public finances, including budgets, tax policy, and borrowing. Their work forms part of fiscal policy: government decisions about raising and spending money. Legislatures approve budgets and taxes under each country's system.

| Institution | Country | Main role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [U.S. Department of the Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/general-information/role-of-the-treasury) | United States | Manages federal finances and government debt, collects revenue through its bureaus, and develops economic and financial policy. |
| [HM Treasury](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-treasury/about) | United Kingdom | Leads government economic and finance policy, including public spending and tax policy. |
| [Ministry of Finance Japan](https://www.mof.go.jp/english/) | Japan | Works on the national budget, taxation, government debt, and international financial policy. |
| [National Treasury](https://www.treasury.gov.za/) | South Africa | Coordinates fiscal policy, the national budget, and management of public finances. |

The Fed and the U.S. Treasury have distinct responsibilities: the Fed makes monetary-policy decisions, while the Treasury manages federal finances and borrowing. Their official role descriptions are linked above.

## International financial institutions and development banks

These bodies pool resources from member countries. The IMF helps countries address economic instability and external payment difficulties. Development banks finance longer-term improvements such as transport, energy, health, and education, through loans, grants, investment, and advice.

| Institution | Main geographic focus | Main role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [International Monetary Fund (IMF)](https://www.imf.org/en/about) | Global | Monitors economies, advises governments, and lends to member countries facing difficulties paying for international obligations. |
| [World Bank Group](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/who-we-are) | Developing economies worldwide | Supports poverty reduction and development through public and private sector financing, guarantees, and knowledge. |
| [African Development Bank Group (AfDB)](https://www.afdb.org/en/about-us/overview) | Africa | Finances development and supports economic and social progress across Africa. |
| [Asian Development Bank (ADB)](https://www.adb.org/who-we-are/about) | Asia and the Pacific | Finances projects and provides advice to support development in the region. |
| [Inter-American Development Bank (IDB)](https://www.iadb.org/en/who-we-are/about-us) | Latin America and the Caribbean | Supports economic and social development through financing and technical assistance. |
| [European Investment Bank (EIB)](https://www.eib.org/en/about/index) | European Union, with operations worldwide | The EU's bank, financing investment that supports EU policy goals, including infrastructure and climate action. |
| [European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD)](https://www.ebrd.com/home/what-we-do.html) | Selected economies across Europe, Asia, and Africa | Invests in businesses and projects and supports reforms that help market economies develop. |
| [Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)](https://www.aiib.org/en/about-aiib/index.html) | Asia and beyond | Finances sustainable infrastructure and regional connections. |
| [Islamic Development Bank (IsDB)](https://www.isdb.org/who-we-are) | Member countries across several regions | Supports development using financing guided by Islamic finance principles. |

The World Bank Group includes several institutions. The World Bank itself comprises the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) and the International Development Association (IDA); the wider group also supports private businesses and investment guarantees. See the [World Bank Group overview](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/who-we-are).

## Global coordination and standards

These bodies help authorities cooperate and develop shared approaches. Their standards and recommendations generally rely on national or regional authorities for implementation.

| Institution | Reach | Main role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bank for International Settlements (BIS)](https://www.bis.org/about) | Global | Supports cooperation among central banks and provides banking services to them. |
| [Financial Stability Board (FSB)](https://www.fsb.org/about/) | Global | Monitors risks to the financial system and coordinates financial authorities' policy work. |
| [Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS)](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/charter) | Global | Develops standards for bank supervision, including how banks manage risks and maintain financial resources to absorb losses. |
| [Financial Action Task Force (FATF)](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/the-fatf/what-we-do.html) | Global | Sets standards against money laundering and terrorist financing and assesses countries' responses. |
| [International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO)](https://www.iosco.org/) | Global | Brings securities regulators together to develop standards and cooperate on market oversight. |

## Financial market regulators

Regulators oversee financial firms or markets and enforce rules within their responsibilities. Securities are financial instruments such as shares and bonds. The bodies below are selected examples; a country may have separate banking, insurance, pensions, and consumer-protection authorities.

| Institution | Jurisdiction | Main role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/about) | United States | Oversees securities markets, protects investors, and supports capital formation: raising money for businesses. |
| [Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)](https://www.fca.org.uk/about) | United Kingdom | Regulates financial firms' conduct and works to protect consumers and market integrity. |
| [European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/about-esma) | European Union | Promotes investor protection and consistent market supervision, alongside direct oversight of certain entities. |
| [Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)](https://www.sebi.gov.in/about-sebi.html) | India | Protects securities investors and regulates and develops India's securities market. |
