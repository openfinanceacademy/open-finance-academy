# Careers

Explore careers in finance, from helping people manage money to building financial technology. These roles offer different ways to work with numbers, solve problems, and support people and organizations.

<div class="career-grid">
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Financial Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Studies financial data, prepares forecasts, and helps organizations make decisions about spending, funding, and growth.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/accountant/' | relative_url }}">Accountant</a></h2>
    <p>Prepares and reviews financial records so people and organizations can understand their income, expenses, and financial position.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/auditor/' | relative_url }}">Auditor</a></h2>
    <p>Examines financial records and processes to check their accuracy and identify weaknesses in how an organization handles money.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-planner/' | relative_url }}">Financial Planner</a></h2>
    <p>Helps individuals and families build plans for saving, managing debt, investing, and working toward long-term financial goals.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/credit-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Credit Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Reviews a borrower's finances and ability to repay debt to help lenders assess loan applications and lending risks.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/investment-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Investment Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Researches companies, markets, and investments to assess potential returns and risks and support investment decisions.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/portfolio-manager/' | relative_url }}">Portfolio Manager</a></h2>
    <p>Selects and monitors a collection of investments based on a client's or organization's goals, time horizon, and tolerance for risk.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/risk-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Risk Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Identifies potential financial losses, estimates their impact, and helps organizations prepare for uncertainty.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/actuary/' | relative_url }}">Actuary</a></h2>
    <p>Uses mathematics and statistics to estimate the likelihood and cost of future events, often for insurance and retirement plans.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/treasury-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Treasury Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Tracks an organization's cash, plans for upcoming payments, and helps ensure money is available when it is needed.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/compliance-officer/' | relative_url }}">Compliance Officer</a></h2>
    <p>Helps a financial organization follow applicable rules and internal policies by reviewing its activities and guiding staff.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-software-developer/' | relative_url }}">Financial Software Developer</a></h2>
    <p>Builds and maintains software for services such as payments, banking, budgeting, and investing.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/investment-banker/' | relative_url }}">Investment Banker</a></h2>
    <p>Helps companies raise money and advises on buying, selling, or combining businesses.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/commercial-banker/' | relative_url }}">Commercial Banker</a></h2>
    <p>Works with businesses to provide loans, accounts, and other banking services.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/insurance-underwriter/' | relative_url }}">Insurance Underwriter</a></h2>
    <p>Evaluates insurance applications and determines coverage terms and pricing.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-trader/' | relative_url }}">Financial Trader</a></h2>
    <p>Buys and sells financial instruments, such as stocks, bonds, and currencies.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/quantitative-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Quantitative Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Uses mathematics, statistics, and programming to model investments and financial risks.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/private-equity-associate/' | relative_url }}">Private Equity Associate</a></h2>
    <p>Evaluates investments in privately owned businesses and supports companies after an investment.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/venture-capital-investor/' | relative_url }}">Venture Capital Investor</a></h2>
    <p>Evaluates young companies and invests in businesses with potential for substantial growth.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/tax-accountant/' | relative_url }}">Tax Accountant</a></h2>
    <p>Prepares tax filings and helps people and organizations understand their tax obligations.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-examiner/' | relative_url }}">Financial Examiner</a></h2>
    <p>Examines financial institutions to assess compliance and financial soundness.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-operations-specialist/' | relative_url }}">Financial Operations Specialist</a></h2>
    <p>Processes transactions, checks records, and helps ensure payments and trades are completed correctly.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/real-estate-financial-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Real Estate Financial Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Evaluates property income, expenses, and financing to assess potential investments.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-counselor/' | relative_url }}">Financial Counselor</a></h2>
    <p>Helps people manage everyday money challenges, including budgeting, debt, and saving.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/public-sector-budget-analyst/' | relative_url }}">Public-Sector Budget Analyst</a></h2>
    <p>Helps government agencies plan budgets, evaluate funding requests, and track spending on public services.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-educator/' | relative_url }}">Financial Educator</a></h2>
    <p>Teaches people how money works and creates learning materials on topics such as budgeting, credit, and investing.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/investor-relations-specialist/' | relative_url }}">Investor Relations Specialist</a></h2>
    <p>Explains a company's financial performance and strategy to investors and analysts through reports, presentations, and meetings.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="career-card">
    <h2><a href="{{ '/careers/financial-technology-product-manager/' | relative_url }}">Financial Technology Product Manager</a></h2>
    <p>Guides the development of financial apps and services by understanding customer needs and coordinating design, engineering, and business teams.</p>
  </article>
</div>
