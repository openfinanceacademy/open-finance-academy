Open Finance Academy is a community-driven project for learning, building, and sharing practical knowledge about open finance.

## Learn in the open

Make financial technology easier to understand, more accessible to build with, and more open to everyone.

## Explore

- **Financial fundamentals**: Build a strong foundation in money, markets, and financial products.
- **Open banking and open finance**: Understand data portability, consent, and interoperability.
- **APIs and data standards**: Learn how financial systems connect and exchange information.
- **Privacy and security**: Build with care and protect people’s financial data.
- **Payments and digital identity**: Explore the systems behind modern financial experiences.
- **DeFi and blockchain**: Examine decentralized financial infrastructure and its tradeoffs.

## Get involved

This project is in its early stages. Suggest a lesson, improve an explanation, share a resource, or build a practical exercise by opening an issue or pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/).
